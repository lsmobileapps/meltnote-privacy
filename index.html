Notes and images on your device

MeltNote works without an account. It stores the notes, checklists, links and images you enter or share with the app in Android's private app storage. It also stores preferences and timestamps for sorting, expiration, recovery and reminders. The developer does not receive this content. The app contains no advertising, analytics or automatic crash reporting service.

When you select, share or take a photo, MeltNote makes a compact copy for the note. It uses Android's camera and photo selection interfaces. Removing MeltNote's copy leaves any selected or shared source image in its original app or library. Photos taken for a note are imported from a temporary file, which MeltNote cleans up.

Reminders and external links

If you set a reminder, MeltNote uses Android notifications to remind you about the note. You can control notification permission and lock screen visibility in Android settings. When you open a link from MeltNote, your browser or another compatible app handles the connection under its own privacy practices.

Age information from Google Play

For applicable Play users, Google Play handles age verification, supervision and age sharing settings. In versions with Play Age Signals, MeltNote asks Google Play whether an age signal is available. When Google shares it, MeltNote briefly checks the age range, its source and the status of approval for significant changes on the device, then discards those details. MeltNote does not read the installation identifier, store the age details, put them in backups or send them to the developer. If Google says verification is required, MeltNote links to Google Play. You can keep using your local notes while you resolve it.

Android backup and device transfer

Your Android backup service can back up note data and preferences depending on your device and settings. Cloud backups can contain image references. They exclude the image files, so some restored notes may show an unavailable image. Supported direct transfers between Android devices can include the image files. Android and your backup provider manage these copies and their retention.

Retention and deletion

A note stays on your device until it expires or you delete it. Notes without an expiration remain until deleted. After expiration or moving a note to Trash, it and its images remain available in Recovery for up to 30 days. You can permanently delete them earlier in Recovery. MeltNote cleans up expired Recovery entries and their local files as it runs.

Clearing the app's storage removes local app data. Android-managed backup copies follow the backup provider's settings and retention rules.
